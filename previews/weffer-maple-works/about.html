About Leonel Weffer

Craftsmanship You Can Trust

Good work speaks for itself. My goal is for every project to feel like it was always part of your home.

Led by Leonel Weffer, Weffer Maple Works focuses on custom carpentry, clear communication, and woodwork designed for real homes.

Leonel brings hands-on experience and a commitment to doing things right: quality materials, clean work, and thoughtful details from the first conversation to the final installation.

Whether it is a built-in, trim package, accent wall, mantel, or one-of-a-kind wood project, every job receives the same level of care and precision.
