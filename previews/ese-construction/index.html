What we install

Residential flooring and the finish work around it. We keep the list short on purpose.

  • Floating floor installation

    Click-together LVP, vinyl plank and laminate — a single room or straight through the main floor.

  • Old flooring removal

    Existing carpet, pad, tack strip and staples removed before the new floor goes down.

  • Underlayment and vapor barrier

    Underlayment or vapor barrier laid over the existing subfloor, as the flooring calls for.

  • Transitions and thresholds

    Clean lines where the new floor meets tile, a doorway, or the next room over.

  • Baseboard, trim and quarter round

    Baseboard and quarter round reset or replaced so the edges finish tight to the wall.

How a job runs

Four steps, start to finish. Which stages apply depends on what your project includes.

  1. 1

    Reach out

    Send the form below or call. Tell us the rooms, your city, and roughly how much floor you're covering.

  2. 2

    Project details and estimate

    We go over the flooring you're considering and which stages the project needs, then put an estimate together before anything gets scheduled.

  3. 3

    Schedule

    We agree on dates and go over what needs to be moved or cleared out of the rooms beforehand.

  4. 4

    Installation and finish

    The stages included in your estimate, run in order — removal, underlayment, plank installation, transitions, and baseboard or trim.
