How to play

  • On your turn, tap Roll Dice to roll four dice.
  • The dice split into two pairs. Each pair's total moves that numbered column up by one. Tap a highlighted pairing button to choose.
  • You may have runners in only 3 columns per turn. Pairings needing a 4th column aren't offered.
  • After a move, tap Roll Again to push your luck, or Stop to bank this turn's progress.
  • If a roll has no legal move, you bust and lose this turn's progress.
  • Reach the top of a column to claim it (★). Claimed columns close to everyone.
  • First player to claim 3 columns wins.
